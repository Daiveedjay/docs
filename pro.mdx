Free includes basic file checks and recurring signed-in project actions. Pro adds continuous enforcement and applicable local project work without the Free monthly action constraint.

Your account shows your current local and cloud allowances. Completed results stay available after exhaustion or expiry, and signed-in custom configuration remains effective. See [Free allowances](/free) for the boundary between a new action and opening an earned result.

A **14-day trial** unlocks everything, and does not require a card.

## What you get

Everything in Free, plus applicable local project work without the Free monthly action constraint and paid automation:

- **Git and build hooks** enforce configured thresholds before a push or build.
- **CI enforcement** applies the configured standard in your pipeline. Automatic cloud triggers are also available to Free accounts within their cloud allowance, after explicit opt-in.
- **Paid cloud allocation** remains separate from local actions; see [Cloud overview](/cloud/overview) and [Scan allowances](/cloud/allowances).
- **Git history secrets scans** and **batch file naming** remain paid workflows.
- **Package guard before push** can check changed lockfiles; the package guard before installation remains Free.

Project scans, [refactor plans](/refactor/overview), Code Map and schema evidence, dependency audits, findings, TODOs and reports are also available to Free accounts within their applicable allowances. Opening earned results is Free. Signed-in custom configuration, scoring weights and Config Studio do not require an unspent project action.

The CLI and [MCP tools](/agents/mcp#tools) keep inspection, saved reads and enforcement separate. `iris check . --inspect` requests a project inspection; `iris gate` enforces a standard. Account Analytics and audit history show recorded activity and do not prove current source passed.

## The trial

Request a 14-day trial from your Iris Code account. Requests are reviewed before activation, so signing in does not start one automatically. Once active you have everything listed above with no card required. After 14 days the account reverts to Free unless you subscribe.

<Tip>
  The most useful way to evaluate the trial is to install a hook and let it block a few real pushes, then check Analytics for the pass/fail counts and the score delta since activation. That gives a concrete picture of what enforcement is catching, which a feature list cannot.
</Tip>

## Upgrading

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the pricing page">
    Visit [iriscode.co/pricing](https://iriscode.co/pricing). Pricing adjusts for your region automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request the trial">
    Click **Request free trial**, sign in with Google or GitHub, and submit from your account page. Pro turns on once the request is approved and you accept the invitation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run the command">
    VS Code and forks: command palette, **Iris Code: Sign In**. JetBrains: **Tools → Iris Code → Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Google or GitHub">
    Your browser opens automatically. There is no separate Iris Code password to create or remember.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to your editor">
    You are redirected back automatically, with no token to copy. One sign-in covers your editors and the `iris` CLI. JetBrains IDEs refresh in place, so no restart is needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pro features activate">
Paid access is confirmed by the server. The sidebar updates its applicable enforcement and local-work permissions; Free actions and saved-result reads remain distinct.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Iris Code never stores your password. Only a licence key is saved: in VS Code's encrypted secret storage, or in a local credentials file shared by JetBrains IDEs and the CLI.
</Note>

## Your subscription

Visit [iriscode.co/account/billing](https://iriscode.co/account/billing) to:

- view your current plan and renewal date
- choose card auto-renew or a one-time bank transfer checkout
- cancel or reactivate a card subscription
- update a payment method if a saved card expires

Cancelling a card subscription retains Pro access until the end of the current billing period.

<Note>
  If you are billed in Nigeria, Iris Code can open a Paystack bank-transfer checkout instead. Paystack generates a transaction-specific account number you can pay from **OPay**, **Kuda**, **Moniepoint**, **PalmPay** or your usual bank app. It expires after about 30 minutes, and a fresh one is generated for the next renewal.
</Note>

## If a payment fails

Paystack retries the charge automatically, and Pro access stays active for **3 days** from the first failed charge. If none of the retries land inside that window, Pro stops and the account reverts to Free.

Iris Code emails you as soon as a charge fails. That email quotes the reason your bank gave and names the date access stops, and the billing screen shows both.

The reason is worth reading before you replace anything: a card with money in it that was refused for a recurring charge is a decision by your bank rather than a problem with the card, and paying once by hand usually clears it.

Your licence key, settings and history survive a lapse. Paying again restores Pro on the same key with nothing to set up.

## Working with a team

Pro covers one developer. If you want the same standard held across a team, [Iris Code Teams](/teams/overview) adds a shared workspace on top: a seat gives every member Pro, one published standard your repositories are measured against, an audit trail a reviewer can read, and a separate paid cloud allowance that scales with seats. The [pricing page](https://iriscode.co/pricing) shows the current configured plan quantities.

Teams is billed per seat from three seats up, and a **14-day trial** with 3 seats needs no card. See [Seats and billing](/teams/seats-and-billing).

<Note>
A paid team seat grants applicable editor and command-line capabilities for that team. Your own repositories use your personal cloud allowance separately, so joining a team does not cancel an existing personal subscription. See [Team entitlement](/teams/entitlement).
</Note>
