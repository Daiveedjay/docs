A seat is a place for one person on your team. Teams is billed monthly for at least **three seats**, using the price and currency for your billing country.

Read this page before adding or removing seats, so you know when the charge changes and when people gain or lose access.

## The price

Every price comes from one price list, based on the billing country stored on the team. Changing a setting cannot move you to a cheaper zone, and the public pricing page reads the same list.

| Zone | Per seat, per month | Smallest team (3 seats) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nigeria | ₦5,000 | ₦15,000 |
| Global | $7 | $21 |
| Emerging markets | $4 | $12 |
| Developing markets | $2 | $6 |

Your team's billing screen shows its zone and currency, with a note that the billing service worked them out.

## Seats, and what holds one

A seat is taken by an **active member** or by a **pending invitation**. Revoking an invitation frees its seat immediately.

That is why an invitation can be refused: five invitations with three seats will not all succeed, and Iris Code tells you which ones did.

## Adding seats

Adding seats takes two steps, so that nobody is charged an amount they have not seen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You are quoted">
    Iris Code works out the charge for the days left in the period you have already paid for, and shows it before anything is charged. The quote separates what is charged now from what your renewal will become.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You confirm">
    The amount you approved is sent back with the request. If the part-period charge has changed since the quote was shown, the change is refused and you are shown a fresh quote to review. A card is never charged an amount nobody saw.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If your team has a card on file, the difference is charged to it straight away and **your renewal date does not move**. If it does not, you are sent to a hosted checkout page instead.

<Note>
  You pay the difference only for the rest of the current period, not a new full month. Charging a whole month would bill you twice for days you have already paid for.
</Note>

## Removing seats

Removing seats is **scheduled for the next renewal**, not applied immediately. The team keeps its current number of seats until then, and the next renewal bills the lower amount.

Nothing is refunded, because the period was already paid for. In return, nobody loses access partway through a period because of a billing change. The billing screen shows the pending reduction and the date it takes effect.

You cannot go below the number of members and pending invitations you actually have, or below the three-seat minimum.

## Renewals

Iris Code charges the card on file automatically each period, for the exact number of seats. You see one charge per period covering every seat.

The team's billing history is on its billing screen, so a finance team can read it without a login to the payment provider.

## If a payment fails

<Steps>
  <Step title="A 7-day grace window opens">
    Every member keeps Pro. Nothing changes for anyone using the product, and the team owner is emailed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Retries happen inside the window">
    A declined card is tried again. A payment whose outcome could not be confirmed is **not** retried until it has been checked with the payment provider, so a card is never charged twice for the same period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After the window, access becomes read-only">
Paid features stop when the grace window ends. The team goes back to whatever Free allowance it has left and its existing choices of active collaborators and projects. Saved reports, membership, history and governance settings are kept. Restoring paid access does not refill the Free allowances underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cancelling

Cancelling stops the next charge, and access continues until the end of the period you have paid for. The billing screen shows the date access ends. You can reactivate before then and nothing is lost.

A team cannot be deleted while a subscription is still running, so cancel first.

## Members who pay for Pro themselves

If a member also has their own Pro subscription, their editor is covered twice. Iris Code says so on their billing screen, rather than letting them discover it on a statement.

That is not always wasted money. A personal subscription pays for cloud scans of the member's **own** repositories, while the seat covers the team's. See [Team entitlement](/teams/entitlement).
