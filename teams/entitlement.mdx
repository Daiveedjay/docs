Being in a team and holding a paid seat are not the same thing, and neither changes your personal account. This page explains what each one gives you, and what happens when you have both.

Three things answer three different questions:

- **Membership** keeps your access to the team's records and its rules for coding agents.
- **Active Free capacity** lets you start new work from the team's shared Free allowance.
- **A paid seat** gives you the Pro features that apply.

Cloud scans of your own repositories always use your personal allowance, separately from any team.

## How Pro is decided

Iris Code checks three things in order and stops at the first that grants Pro:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Personal Pro subscription">
    An active personal subscription. Reported as **individual**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Personal Pro trial">
    An active 14-day Pro trial. Also reported as **individual**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active team seat">
    Reported as **team**, along with which team is covering you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A seat is checked **last**, and only when nothing personal already grants Pro. Joining a team therefore changes nothing for someone who already pays for Pro.

## What a seat gives you

Everything in Pro, in your editor and CLI, for as long as the team's subscription runs. Your account page names the team covering you and the date its cover currently runs to.

It also gives you access to that team: its connected repositories, its reports, its audit trail and its scan allowance.

## What a seat does not give you

**Cloud scans of your own repositories.** Your account and each team have separate scan allowances, and a team seat does not provide scans for your personal account.

Personal repositories use your personal cloud allowance, including the recurring Free allowance where it is available. A paid team seat covers work in that team only. It does not buy extra personal cloud scans.

<Warning>
  This is why Iris Code does not cancel a personal subscription when you join a team, and does not ask you to. Cancelling would quietly stop your own repositories being scanned, and a team seat does not cover them.
</Warning>

## If you have both

Your editor is covered by your personal subscription, and the seat gives you access to the team. Your billing screen shows a notice saying you are covered twice, so you do not first find out from a bank statement.

Keeping both makes sense if you work on your own repositories as well as the team's. If you only ever work on the team's, cancelling your personal subscription stops that charge without affecting your access to the team.

## Two teams

Only one seat provides your editor's Pro access, and a **paid** team always wins over one on a trial. A trial ends after a fortnight while a subscription carries on, so choosing the paid team means your Pro lasts as long as someone is actually paying for it.

Scan allowances work differently: you can use both teams' pools, each on that team's own repositories.

## When cover ends

### You are removed from the team

Cover ends immediately. Iris Code tells you as it happens and returns you to your personal account. If your Pro came from that seat, it ends with it; if you pay for Pro yourself, nothing changes.

That team's notifications are cleared from your inbox at the same time, because every link in them pointed somewhere you can no longer open.

### The team stops paying

Every member keeps Pro for a 7-day grace period. After that, the team's paid access becomes read-only and members drop to Free together. Existing reports stay readable. See [Seats and billing](/teams/seats-and-billing).

### The team is deleted

Cover ends with it, and its repositories, reports and audit trail are removed. Your personal account is untouched. Deleting your last team returns you to it.

### Your seat is deactivated but you stay a member

A seat that is not active does not grant Pro. Ask an owner or admin to reactivate it; the team needs an unused seat for that.

## What your account page shows

Your billing screen looks at all of your cover, not just your own subscription, and shows one of three cases:

- **Paying yourself** - your plan, price and renewal date, plus the "covered twice" notice if you also hold a seat
- **Covered by a team** - which team, until when, that there is nothing for you to pay, and that a personal subscription is what would audit your own repositories
- **Neither** - the Free plan, with the upgrade path

<Note>
  Your editor and the website decide whether you have Pro using the same logic, so they cannot disagree.
</Note>
